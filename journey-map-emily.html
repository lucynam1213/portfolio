Actions
Emily opens two apps every morning — one for meal logging, one for workouts. She manually notes whether diet affected performance in a separate memo. It's tedious and she skips it often.
A colleague mentions FitBridge's combined tracking. As a designer, Emily critically reviews the UI before committing — she checks screenshots, reads reviews, and explores the onboarding demo.
Emily uses photo scan to log breakfast, then logs her morning workout. For the first time, both appear on a single dashboard. She pauses to explore the combined nutrition and activity view.
After two weeks, Emily opens the weekly summary and sees her nutrition and workout data in one chart. She notices her low-protein days correlate with weaker session performance.
FitBridge has fully replaced her two previous apps. Morning meal logging takes under 30 seconds with photo scan. She checks her weekly digest every Sunday as a new ritual.
