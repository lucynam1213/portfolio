Actions
Alex's trainer mentions FitBridge. He downloads it on his commute, skeptically skimming the features and comparing it to apps he's stopped using before.
He creates a profile, connects with his trainer remotely, and receives his first assigned weekly workout plan with clear exercises and video guides.
Alex follows the assigned workouts for two weeks. He uses the quick-log feature after each session and gets push reminders on rest days.
He opens the progress screen and sees a 4-week streak. His trainer sends an encouraging message through in-app messaging, reinforcing his effort.
After missing a session, Alex receives a trainer check-in. He logs a make-up workout and reschedules using the app's weekly planner.
