Shopcord Subscription, Shopify Integration

Onboarding FashionGo vendors onto a new subscription product — from Shopify connection to billing

Role:Product Designer Company:NHN Global Model:3-month free trial, then paid subscription
New Product Subscription Billing Onboarding Shopify App

The Challenge

Shopcord is a new subscription product from NHN Global, built for FashionGo's vendor customers. It turns a vendor's product catalog into digital linesheets and catalog books, lets vendors share them with buyers directly over SMS, and lets buyers purchase a vendor's items straight through Shopcord. Because it's a brand-new product rather than an extension of something buyers and vendors already knew, two problems had to be solved from scratch and fairly ambiguously at the start: getting a vendor's Shopify store connected and onboarded with as little friction as possible, and designing a subscription billing system — Shopcord offers a 3-month free trial before converting to a paid monthly plan — that stays clear and trustworthy through every edge case: expired cards, declined payments, grace periods, cancellation, even chargebacks.

Figma Shopify App Patterns Design Systems
01

A Guided, 5-Step Shopify Connection

Connecting a vendor's Shopify store to Shopcord means leaving our product, creating a developer app inside Shopify's own admin, copying credentials, and coming back — a lot of surface area for a vendor to get lost in. I designed a 5-step wizard (Create app → URL & Access → Connect to Shopcord → Install app → Settings) with inline screenshots annotated to match Shopify's actual admin UI at each step, plus explicit error states for when a store name or credential doesn't match.

02

Matching Credentials Back to the Source

Step 3 asks the vendor to paste their store name and API credentials from Shopify's Dev Dashboard. I paired each input field with a cropped, highlighted screenshot showing exactly where that value lives in Shopify's own interface, and built inline validation that tells the vendor immediately if a field is missing rather than failing silently after submission.

03

Designing for Every Billing State

Beyond the happy path of an active subscription, I mapped and designed every state a vendor's billing can be in: free trial, active, card expiring, card limit reached, payment declined with a countdown to retry, grace period, cancelling, cancelled, and even chargeback. Each state surfaces the right message and the right next action — update a card, restore billing, export a receipt — directly on the Subscription & Billing page, so a vendor is never left guessing why their account looks different.
