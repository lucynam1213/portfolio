02 / 03   The Problem

Ten years of
growth, zero
coherence.

FashionGo had grown organically for a decade. Different teams built different pages with different patterns — buyers and vendors faced a fragmented, inconsistent experience.

The challenge: bring coherence without breaking what 150,000 buyers depend on every day.

"We had a product that worked — but felt like five different products wearing the same logo."

UI audit — 200+ screens mapped before a single new pixel was drawn

03 / 03   Solution

Brand-first.
Not category-
first.

The biggest insight from research: wholesale buyers shop by brand loyalty, not by category. The old platform forced a category-first mental model.

The redesign flipped this — brand storefronts as the primary unit, with persistent order context so buyers never lose their place mid-session.

Checkout steps reduced from 7 to 3.

After — new navigation hierarchy
