About Me

LN
Lucy Nam
Product Designer

I'm a Product Designer with over 8 years of experience designing scalable digital products across complex B2B and eCommerce ecosystems. I hold an MA in Interaction Design from the Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD). I specialize in building and evolving design systems that bring clarity, cohesion, and efficiency to cross-functional teams. My work sits at the intersection of craft and systems thinking — translating business complexity into structured, accessible, and visually refined experiences.

Currently, I lead UI and design system initiatives at a large-scale eCommerce platform, collaborating closely with product, engineering, and brand stakeholders. I'm passionate about elevating design maturity, strengthening collaboration, and building foundations that empower teams to move faster with confidence.
