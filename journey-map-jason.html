Actions
Jason signs up for a gym membership but skips sessions within two weeks. He tells himself he'll start again Monday — but Monday comes and goes.
A coworker mentions FitBridge's built-in accountability features. Jason is skeptical but intrigued — the app promises simplicity and reminds him without nagging.
Jason sets up a basic three-session weekly plan and enables push reminders. He invites his coworker as an accountability partner in two taps.
His phone buzzes before each session. The plan is short and clear — no guesswork needed. He completes a full week of workouts for the first time in months.
Jason hits 10 consecutive check-ins. Streak alerts and his accountability partner's reactions keep him coming back. Missing now feels worse than going.

Design Insight

Design Insight
Partner accountability is the core differentiator. Make the invite flow feel social, not clinical — one tap, instant connection.
Design Insight
Smart reminders timed to past behavior: "You usually go at 7pm on Tuesdays." Reduce the decision to just showing up.
Design Insight
